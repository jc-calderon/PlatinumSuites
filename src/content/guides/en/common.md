## Kitchen and laundry {#cocina}

The kitchen is fully equipped. The **microwave**, **oven** and **range hood** have touch controls: for best results, use the whole pad of your finger with a gentle touch.

### How to use the microwave {+}

1. Press **O**.
2. Press **F**.
3. Select the power level (10–100).
4. Press **OK**.
5. Select the time.
6. Press **OK**.

To cancel, use the **C/** button.

> [!NOTE]
> The microwave keeps its fan running for about **2 minutes** after use, even when switched off. This is normal.

### Washing machine

Your suite has a **washing machine**.

## Pool and common areas {#piscina}

Enjoy the **infinity pool** on the **15th floor**, with panoramic views of Cochabamba. It is perfect for relaxing, sunbathing or cooling off.

## House rules {#reglas}

### Apartment rules

- **No pets.** Pets of any kind are not allowed.
- **No parties.** Please respect the peace and quiet of our neighborhood.
- **No smoking.** Smoking inside the apartment is strictly prohibited.
- **Quiet hours: 11:00 PM to 8:00 AM.** Please keep noise to a minimum.
- **Occupancy: maximum {{capacity}} guests**{{capacityNote}}. Please respect the guest limit for a safe stay.
- **No alcoholic beverages.** Drinking alcohol is not allowed.

### Building rules

- Only guests **registered in advance** with a valid ID may enter the apartment.
- Under building policy, **visitors are not allowed** inside the apartment. You are welcome to receive visitors in the **lobby**.

## Trash and recycling {#basura}

The **trash room** is in the **semi-basement**: in the elevator, press **E1**. It is open **24 hours**.

- **PET bottles:** in the basket provided for them.
- **Cardboard:** separately.
- **Everything else:** in the trash bins.

## Before you leave {#antes-de-irse}

- **Tidy up.** Tidy and clean the space. Wash the dishes you used, take the trash to the trash room (semi-basement, **E1** button), separating PET bottles and cardboard, and return items to where you found them.
- **Double-check your belongings.** Check drawers, closets, charging outlets and bathroom toiletries.
- **Turn off appliances.** Make sure all appliances, lights and electronic devices are off, including those in the kitchen.
- **Close all doors and windows** to keep the property secure.
- **Check-out by {{checkout}}.** Simply close the door when you leave.
